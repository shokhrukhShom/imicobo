Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

iMicobo turns your phone into a wireless karaoke microphone and shows song lyrics on your screen. This policy explains the small amount of data the service touches and how it is handled. Plain version first: we don't record your singing, and we don't sell your data.

Your voice and audio

When you pair a phone, your microphone audio streams directly to the screen you're singing on using a peer-to-peer WebRTC connection. Our signaling server only helps the two devices find each other — it passes the short connection-setup messages (network addresses and codec details) needed to open the link. Your audio is never recorded, stored, or listened to by us. Pairing codes are temporary and expire shortly after use.

When you buy a pass

iMicobo is free for 5 songs a day. To sing unlimited you can buy a Day Pass or a 3-Month Pass. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number — Stripe handles that. From a completed purchase we receive and store:

  • Your email address (used to identify your pass and sign you in).
  • Which pass you bought and when it expires.
  • Whether you opted in to marketing email.

Stripe's handling of your payment is governed by the Stripe Privacy Policy.

Signing in

There are no passwords. After you buy a pass, we set a signed cookie in your browser so the service remembers your pass on that device. The cookie contains your email and a signature — nothing else — and lasts up to 180 days. You can clear it any time by clearing your browser cookies.

Stored in your browser only

Your count of free songs for the day is kept in your browser's local storage and resets at midnight your local time. It never leaves your device and is not sent to us.

Marketing email

We'll only email you promotions if you check the opt-in box at checkout. It's unchecked by default. Every such email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out any time by contacting us.

Other services on the page

  • YouTube — karaoke videos play through YouTube's embedded player, which is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple iTunes Search — used to fetch album artwork for songs. No personal data is sent.

What we do not do

  • We don't record, store, or share your microphone audio.
  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't use advertising trackers.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account data (email and purchase record) or unsubscribe from email by contacting us at the address below. Note that payment records Stripe is legally required to keep are managed by Stripe under their policy.

Children

iMicobo is a general-audience karaoke service and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Material changes will be noted on this page.
